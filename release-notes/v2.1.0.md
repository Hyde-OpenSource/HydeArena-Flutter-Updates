Hyde Arena 2.1.0 — staff logins, credit, shop open/close and more

IMPORTANT AFTER UPDATING
• Sign in with username "admin" and your existing password.
• Press "Open shop" (top right) before starting work each day. Everything until you close the shop counts for that day, even after midnight.

NEW
• Staff logins: Admin and Manager roles, plus your own custom roles. Managers can run sessions and restock but cannot see earnings or change prices.
• Credit (pay later): take part or all of a bill on credit, see who owes what, and record payments. Switch it on in Settings → Features.
• Split payments: Cash + UPI on one bill (and any mix, if switched on).
• Add or remove controllers during a session; the new rate applies from that minute.
• 45-minute slot, per-minute pricing, fixed-booking-only consoles and minimum charges.
• Edit, delete and restore transactions (deleted ones go to a bin).
• Activity log: who did what and when, in Settings → Activity log.
• Walk-in name and phone when saving a session.
• Settings → Features: switch off anything your café does not use.

Your data is backed up automatically before the update installs.
