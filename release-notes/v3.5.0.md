Hyde Arena 3.5 — cash drawer, session billing, data cleanup and automatic updates

BEFORE UPDATING
• The database is copied before its schema is upgraded. Normal backups remain in Documents\HydeArena\Backups.
• Review the new “Manage cash drawer” permission in Settings → Users & roles. Admins have it; existing roles with finance and transaction editing access inherit it.

NEW
• Cash drawer in Transactions: record cash added with its source, emergency expenses with a reason, and transfers to or from change. Expenses reduce the day's main cash collection. Moving money into change reduces collection by the same amount; moving it back increases collection. Change carries forward across shop close and reopen.
• Edit or remove a drawer movement during its business day. Reports show cash expenses and additions separately from sales revenue. The close-shop screen shows main collection and carried change.
• Fixed sessions can now be billed by the exact booked minutes at the listed console rate, even when package prices exist. The selected method remains in effect when time is added or deducted.
• Security → Clear historical data: choose a date range and record categories, preview the counts, confirm with your password, and save a full backup before removal. Running work, customer balances and current inventory stay in place.
• Updates are checked after sign-in and every six hours. An available release opens a dialog; you can skip that version, retry a failed check, or turn on automatic verified downloading. Installation still asks you first and takes a backup.
• Window controls are available in the app header. Initial window size now fits the visible desktop.

UPDATING
• The Windows installer and SHA-256 file must both be attached to the GitHub release v3.5.0.
