Hyde Arena 4.2.5 — denser checkout screens, notes at checkout, saved sessions and snacks on one page

SELL SCREEN
• The current order is redesigned to show more at once: one slim row to choose counter sale, session or waiting; one line per item; payment methods as a single row of small buttons.
• "Paid extra → wallet", "Attach customer" and "Discount / custom price" are short links under the payment methods and open only when needed.
• The total line also shows how many items are in the order, or what is still missing ("Choose how it is paid").
• Items no longer get squeezed out of view on a short window.

SESSION CHECKOUT
• The Collect button stays at the bottom of the window; only the bill and the choices above it scroll.
• Payment methods are a single row of small buttons, and the bill summary is more compact, so the whole checkout fits without scrolling in most windows.
• "Note on receipt" is now a note box for the session: type, choose public or private with the small icon, and press Enter or the send button to save it. Anything left in the box is saved as a note at checkout. (With Notes switched off it stays a receipt note.)
• The newest notes of the session show on the left, under the bill, on every tab. Click them to see all notes or add one. The separate "Add note" button is gone.

SAVED SESSIONS & SNACKS
• The Saved page now shows both saved sessions and saved snack orders, with one search for both and the amount still to collect.
• Collect a snack order, put it on a session bill, or cancel it from that page. The "Saved orders" button on the Sell screen opens it.
• The menu badge counts both.
