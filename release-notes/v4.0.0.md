Hyde Arena 4.0 — per-minute billing everywhere, recover a checkout, finer permissions, notes and search

BEFORE UPDATING
• The database is copied before its schema is upgraded (schema 7). Normal backups remain in Documents\HydeArena\Backups.
• Roles keep everything they could already do: each role receives the new, finer permissions that grew out of the ones it had. Review Settings → Users & roles afterwards and untick what a role should lose.
• Floor roles (anyone who can start sessions) can read and write notes. Switch Notes off in Settings → Features if you do not want it.

PER-MINUTE BILLING
• Settings → Features → "Bill fixed sessions per minute": every timed session starts at zero and the bill grows each minute played, instead of charging the booked time up front.
• Consoles → edit → "Bill every fixed session per minute": the same rule for one console or table only.
• Sessions started from the waiting list or from a booking now follow these settings. The "Bill this booking per minute" switch on the start screen still overrides them for one session.
• A console's minimum charge is shown on the start screen when it applies to a per-minute session.

RECOVER A CHECKED-OUT SESSION
• Checked out by mistake? Open the bill in Transactions and press "Recover session". Put the session back on a free console or table of the same game, or save it for later.
• Choose whether the time since the checkout counts as played, and whether the money collected is kept as an advance or removed.
• The bill moves to the Deleted bin; the customer's spend, credit, wallet and time bank are put back; snacks and advances stay on the session.
• Open and per-minute sessions checked out on an older version cannot be recovered.

PERMISSIONS
• "Pause, extend & move" is now five permissions: Pause & resume, Add time, Change controllers or players, Move to another console, Save & exit.
• Wallet: Take advances & keep excess, Top up wallets, Give wallet money back. Untick "Top up wallets" to stop top-ups while still keeping the excess of a bill.
• Transactions: Add & edit, Delete & restore, and Recover checked-out sessions.
• Notes: See notes, Write notes, Manage everyone's notes, See everyone's private notes.

PLAYERS AND CONTROLLERS MID-SESSION
• Each change can be counted three ways, chosen on the spot with the amount shown: from this minute, per started hour, or the whole session at the new rate. The usual way is set in Settings → Sessions & alerts.
• FIXED: a player leaving with "per started hour" could take a full hour off the bill. Only the time they were gone is taken off now.
• FIXED: adding a player to a fixed booking ignored package prices. The package difference is charged now.
• FIXED: "per started hour" could charge more time than the booking had.
• The top snooker tier is labelled "5+" (it was "4+", next to "4").

NOTES
• New Notes page: notes for the whole team or private to their author, with pinning, search and filters.
• Notes tab on every running or saved session.
• Private notes are seen only by their author, and by roles allowed to see everyone's private notes.

SEARCH
• Search added to Saved sessions, the waiting list, bookings (upcoming and past), and rentals (out now and history).
