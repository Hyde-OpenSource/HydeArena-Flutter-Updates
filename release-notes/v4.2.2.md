Hyde Arena 4.2.2 — a faster Sell screen you can arrange

CHECKOUT ON THE SELL SCREEN
• The payment methods no longer scroll away with the items. Cash, UPI, Card and the split choices stay in view above the total, so a sale is: tap the products, tap the method, Complete sale.
• Only the list of items scrolls. The total, "Complete sale" and "Save for later" always stay at the bottom, side by side so they fit a short window.
• Discount and custom price sit behind one small button until they are needed.

ARRANGE THE SELL SCREEN
• Drag products into the order you want: press the arrange button beside the search box, drag a product onto another to put it in that place, then press ✓.
• The layout menu beside it sets the product tile size (small, medium, large), the product order (my order, A to Z, price), and whether the current order sits on the right or the left.
• The bar between the products and the order can still be dragged to resize them.
• Arranging needs "Change settings" or "Manage products & prices". The arrangement is shared by everyone on that PC. "Reset to standard" undoes it.

This release also contains 4.2.1: no payment method when starting a session, corrections from the customer page, and filters on Stock & profit.
