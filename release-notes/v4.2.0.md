Hyde Arena 4.2 — saved snack orders and inventory search

SAVED SNACK ORDERS
• Snacks & POS → Sell → "Save for later (pay afterwards)": keep a counter order open instead of taking the money now. Attach a customer, type a name, or leave it for nobody.
• The items leave the stock when the order is saved, so the shelf count stays right.
• "Saved orders" at the top of the current order lists every open one, with search. For each: Collect (any payment method, wallet or credit), Put on a session bill (running or saved session, paid at its checkout), or Cancel order (items go back into stock, with a reason).
• To put snacks straight onto a session, "Bill a session" works as before.
• Switch it off in Settings → Features → Modules → Saved snack orders.

SEARCH
• Inventory: search products by name. Type "low" or "out" to see what needs reordering.

This release also contains everything from 4.1: choose the payment method every time (or set a default), customise the dashboard, side menu modes, one-step notes on sessions and the new wallet permissions.
