Hyde Arena 3.0 — snooker, packages, advances, stock & profit, waiting list, bookings and rentals

IMPORTANT AFTER UPDATING
• Your data is upgraded automatically. A copy of your database from before the upgrade is saved in Documents\HydeArena\Backups (HydeArena_BeforeUpgrade_schema3.db).
• Existing roles keep what they could do. New permissions were added next to the closest old one (for example "Edit session start time" next to "Deduct time"). Check Settings → Users & roles if you want to change who gets what.
• Set purchase prices on your products (Snacks & POS → Inventory → Edit) so profit is worked out correctly.

NEW
• Snooker: add snooker tables next to your consoles. They are priced by players (2P, 3P, 4P, 4P+). The dashboard has game buttons (All / PS5 / Snooker) and a New session button.
• Console colours: give every console or table its own colour.
• Time slots you control: Settings → Time slots & packages. Change the duration buttons for each game, choose the preselected one, or give a console its own list.
• Package prices: "30 minutes = ₹100, 1 hour = ₹180". Set a price per slot and tier; other durations are rounded up to the next package or charged pro-rata, as you choose. Open sessions use them too.
• Per-minute or per-hour billing for every console: played 15 minutes = 15 minutes charged (updating every minute), or whole started hours.
• Advance payments: take part or full payment before a session, booking, waiting person or rental. It shows as PAID / advance, and is deducted at checkout. Unused advance is refunded or kept on the customer's wallet.
• Customer wallet: extra money received at checkout is stored on the customer and can pay any later bill. Top up, spend and give back from the customer's Wallet tab.
• Custom prices: agree a price when starting a booking, or "Charge exactly" any amount at checkout, POS or rental return. Every change is logged.
• Edit the start time of a running session (started late or too early). Time and bill are worked out again; a fixed booking keeps its price.
• Quick time menu on every console tile: add 15 / 30 / 60 minutes, deduct 5 / 10 / 15 with a reason, or change the start time, without opening the checkout window.
• Checkout for saved sessions: end a saved session without resuming it, add snacks to it, or discard it.
• Void a session that has snacks: choose to return the snacks to stock, or to void the play and still charge the snacks.
• Credit customers: pick the customer from a searchable list (with what they already owe) or add a new customer on the spot.
• Waiting list: queue people for a console or any table of a game. They can order snacks and pay in advance while they wait; the dashboard reminds you when the console they want is ending or over time and shows other consoles with their remaining time so you can seat them elsewhere.
• Bookings: reserve a console for later with advance payment and snacks. Reminders before the start, overlap checks and a timeline of the day.
• Rentals: rent out a console or anything else. Record the renter's details and ID, rent, deposit, what was paid and what is left, and the return time. The dashboard reminds you before and after it is due, with the late fee.
• Stock & profit: every stock change is recorded (what, when, how many, at what price, by whom). Restock with the purchase price, record wastage and count corrections, see the history, and export a full Excel workbook.
• Profit in Reports: revenue, cost of goods, profit and margin, revenue by game, profit by day and by product, and an Excel export.
• Snacks & POS: a much bigger billing panel that you can resize, bigger inventory rows, purchase price and margin per product.
• New permission "See earnings on Transactions": people without it still see the list of transactions, but not the totals or the export.

Every new feature can be switched off in Settings → Features.
