Hyde Arena 3.5.1 — per-minute billing for fixed sessions

FIXED
• "Bill this booking per minute" now really bills every minute played, the same way an open session does. The bill starts at zero and grows each minute at the console's listed rate. A customer who leaves early pays only for the minutes played, and overtime keeps adding minute by minute. The booked duration only sets the timer and the reminders.
• Adding time to a per-minute booking costs nothing up front; the minutes are billed as they are played. Deducting time takes those minutes off the bill.
• Changing controllers on a per-minute booking bills the new rate from that minute, like an open session.
• A custom price cannot be combined with per-minute billing; the agreed price is used.

BEFORE UPDATING
• Check out any running session that was started with "Bill this booking per minute" on the old version. Otherwise it is billed twice: once for the booking price it already holds and once for the minutes played.
