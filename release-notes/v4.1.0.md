Hyde Arena 4.1 — choose the payment method every time, your own dashboard, side menu modes, quick notes

BEFORE UPDATING
• No payment method is preselected any more. Staff choose Cash, UPI, Card (or a split) on every bill, advance and wallet movement, and the button stays disabled until they do. To get the old behaviour back, set Settings → Sessions & alerts → "Payment method selected by default" to Cash.
• Roles keep what they could do: the two new wallet permissions are given to every role that could already do the same thing. Review Settings → Users & roles afterwards.

PAYMENT METHOD
• Settings → Sessions & alerts → "Payment method selected by default": None (choose every time), Cash, UPI or Card.
• Applies to session checkout, counter sales, advances, deposits, rentals, credit payments, time bank recharges, wallet top-ups and refunds, and when starting a session.
• Checkout no longer preselects the method picked when the session started.

DASHBOARD
• Customise the dashboard (small button at the bottom right of the dashboard, or Settings → Sessions & alerts → Dashboard; needs "Change settings").
• Numbers (today's sales, credit, consoles in use, sessions today, needs attention, saved sessions): show or hide each one, change the order, make each small, medium or large, and place the row above or below the consoles.
• Side panels (reminders, needs attention, recent payments): show or hide, reorder, resize, put them left or right of the consoles and choose their width.
• Console tiles: small, medium or large.
• "Reset to standard" brings back the built-in arrangement. Numbers and panels a user has no permission for stay hidden for that user.

SIDE MENU
• Three modes: always open, open on hover (icons only until the mouse is over it), and icons only. Switch with the small button beside the version number (or at the top of the icon strip), or in Settings → Sessions & alerts → Side menu.

NOTES
• Add a note to a running session in one step: the note button on its dashboard tile, or "Add note" in the session window. Type, choose public or private, save.
• The tile's note button is filled when the session already has notes.

WALLET PERMISSIONS
• "Keep money on wallets": store money on a customer wallet at a bill (the extra a customer paid, or an unused advance).
• "Pay bills from wallets": take money out of a customer wallet to pay a bill. Until now anyone taking a payment could do this.
• These join "Top up wallets" and "Give wallet money back", so each way money enters or leaves a wallet has its own permission. "Take advances" now covers advances only.
