Hyde Arena 4.2.1 — no payment method when starting a session, corrections from the customer page, stock filters

STARTING A SESSION
• The payment method is no longer asked when a session starts. Money is taken at checkout, and that is where the method is chosen.
• Taking an advance: type the amount and the payment method choices appear beside it. This applies to every advance and deposit field (sessions, bookings, waiting list, rentals).
• Members with prepaid time get a "Pay from time bank" switch on the start screen instead of a payment tile.

CUSTOMERS
• Credit, Wallet and payment history: tap any entry to open it, or use the edit and delete buttons on its row. What the customer owes, their wallet and their lifetime spend update on the spot.
• "Add credit" on the Credit tab records credit that was never entered, with what it was for and when.
• Editing needs "Add & edit transactions"; deleting needs "Delete & restore transactions".

STOCK & PROFIT
• By product: search, quick filters (Sold, Not sold, Bought in, Adjusted or wasted, Out of stock, Sold at a loss) and a minimum / maximum on any column (margin in percent).
• Click a column heading to sort by it, largest or smallest first.
• A totals row sums the products shown.
